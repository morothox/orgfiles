* Make America Burn Again? – The Drastic Result of Negligence of the Environment

The Earth's temperature has soared over the last few years; therefore, many people, including environmental experts, have been worried about how the planet is developing. In contrast, people like Donald Trump deny any man-made catastrophes caused by climate change. Trump neglects these facts and even takes actions against protecting the environment by reversing the Paris Climate Treaty.

The US has already been hit by different climate-related problems, including the spread of various diseases transmitted by mosquitoes. Moreover, the country has endured serious wildfires in California, which cost around 400 billion dollars that the taxpayer had to pay. The sectors that are most affected by this are tourism and agriculture. When asked about California, the President responded by advising Californians to take care of the forest floor. He claimed this right after he had spoken to the Finnish head of state, who told Trump how the Finns were managing their forests. Therefore, Trump concluded that since Finland does not have any wildfire problems, America should just do the same. Many firefighters denied Trump's claim, stating that his suggestion would not help, as the fire was caused by climate change. The US government has to provide a climate report to Congress, informing them about the dangers and problems related to the environmental changes which happened in the United States. The President, however, did not care about this document and accused the researchers behind it of following a political agenda. Moreover, he even canceled the Paris Climate Treaty and spoke jokingly about global warming.

In conclusion, the facts are clear: the negative effects of climate change are already appearing right in the US. Many experts have proven this countless times, but some people like Donald Trump will not accept this and continue joking about serious topics like wildfires and diseases.
